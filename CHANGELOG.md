- **08 Nov** — Local-first knowledge + official-record checks. **Root causes found by a 67-case stress test:** the search index served 11 wrong IPC→BNS pairs from a hand-typed table (e.g. IPC 304 → "BNS 80", dowry death) and re-running the corpus script overwrote the verified table; the verified table had IPC 326B → 125 (Gazette: 124(2)); all 15 SC Monitor seed rulings were fabricated (checked against the official record: 10 citations belong to other cases, 5 don't exist) — removed; scraped rulings never reached the model; LEXI chat ignored the provider setting; Ollama ran with its default 2–4K context and silently cut the prompt. **Knowledge now local:** full Gazette text of BNS/BNSS/BSA + BNSS First Schedule classification, POCSO, 2,281 Supreme Court criminal judgments 2014–2025 (CC-BY open dataset) as "similar real cases", and the official SC citation record (38,139 neutral citations). **Search:** local Qwen3-Embedding 4B, plain-language search keys, two-tier pools, local issue summary in the query, definition→punishment expansion — required sections retrieved 59% → 94.4% on the stress set. **Generation:** `LLM_PROVIDER=hybrid` (local draft → verifier → Groq fixes rejected drafts); measured qwen3:8b is weaker than gpt-oss-120b (4/6 vs 14/14 on hard cases), so Groq stays the default writer. **Checks:** non-existent sections, number↔offence label mismatches, BNS 106(2) (not in force), SC citations not in the official record or belonging to another case. Look-alike guidance + ingredient test cut wrong extra charges. `tools/add_special_act.py` adds other special laws from official PDFs (refuses outdated copies). **Embedding cache:** local-model vectors (~180 MB) now live in a gitignored `embedding_cache.local.json`; the committed Gemini cache stays small, and the cache is read from disk once per process instead of on every query. `tools/prewarm_embeddings.py` warms exactly what the index embeds. 519 tests.

- **02 Nov** [`f90c5f3`](https://github.com/Yuvraj235/Lex-Indic-legal/commit/f90c5f3) — Accuracy + hardening round 2. **Model:** Groq retired `llama-3.3-70b-versatile`, so every analysis was failing; the model is now `GROQ_MODEL` (default `openai/gpt-oss-120b`, chosen on the 10-scenario pipeline eval) behind one helper that handles each model family's quirks and normalises output (e.g. the narrow no-break space gpt-oss puts between "Section" and the number). **Accuracy:** `statute_lookup.py` answers IPC→BNS / bailable / punishment questions from the tables (15/15 on the knowledge eval vs 2/15 for the local fine-tunes); the verifier now also catches spelled-out citations, IPC↔BNS pairings that contradict the concordance, and case law that is unverified, overruled or wrongly reported; `/analyze` and `/api/v1/analyze` get one targeted self-correction pass when the verifier finds problems; section headings parse in markdown/dash variants. **Hardening:** security headers (CSP frame-ancestors, nosniff, Referrer/Permissions-Policy, HSTS over https), 25 MB request cap, cross-site write refusal, logout revokes every outstanding session token. Citations written with the section sign ("BNS §85", the way gpt-oss writes them) are now recognised — before, such answers passed the checker with nothing checked; IPC 498A now pairs with both BNS 85 and 86. **Result:** 10/10 scenarios, mean 0.99 → 1.00 after the 498A fix, 0 invented sections (gpt-oss-120b) vs mean 0.95 and 5 invented sections across 10 (qwen3.8-27b). **Eval tooling:** base-number grading (103(2) is section 103), wrong-pairing / bad-precedent columns, `--out-dir`, raw outputs saved to outputs/eval/.
- **02 Nov** [`f90c5f3`](https://github.com/Yuvraj235/Lex-Indic-legal/commit/f90c5f3) — Security hardening from the technical audit. **Access control:** case files (`/history`, `/load`, `/download`) belong to their owner/firm, with signed 24 h download tokens for anonymous runs; matters, lawyers, conflict checks, webhooks and monitors are scoped to the caller's firm (anonymous callers get 401); joining a firm needs a partner's single-use invite (`/auth/invites` → `/auth/bind-firm`), role taken from the invite; API keys can only be revoked by their owner and self-service rates are capped; NALSA export, WhatsApp inbound (Twilio signature / shared token) and stub billing (dev-only) no longer open by default. **Secrets:** new `envmode.py` — production is the default, the app refuses to boot without `SECRET_KEY`/`AUDIT_HASH_SALT`, no built-in fallback keys, login codes only echoed under `LEX_ENV=development`; admin gate fails closed, header-only, constant-time. **Abuse:** 5 wrong guesses burn a login code, 5 codes/email/hour, 10/min/IP; anonymous `/chat` and `/tabular` capped per IP; `ProxyFix` + `TRUST_PROXY_HOPS` replace trusting raw `X-Forwarded-For`. **Webhooks:** https-only, public-IP targets only (checked again at delivery), no redirects, per-subscription signing secrets. **Privacy:** `EMBEDDINGS_PROVIDER=ollama` for local embeddings, `/llm/status` reports `fully_local`, corrected "zero data leaves" claims, ChromaDB telemetry off. **Bugs:** same-second case files/monitors overwrote each other, matter IDs could collide across firms, `PATCH …/status` never matched a real matter id, erasure email never sent (`mailer_module` undefined), `/status` self-probe hit a hard-coded port. Also: model output HTML-escaped, CORS allowlist instead of `*`, bounded LLM retries (503 instead of minute-long sleeps), RSS DTDs refused, dev server binds 127.0.0.1. 388 tests (99 of them in the new `tests/test_security.py`).
