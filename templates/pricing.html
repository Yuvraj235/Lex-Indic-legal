FAQ

Can I switch tiers later?
Yes — upgrade or downgrade anytime. Prorated billing handles the difference automatically.
Do you offer a money-back guarantee?
14-day refund, no questions asked. Just email us within 14 days of your first charge.
Why is NALSA free forever?
NALSA panel advocates handle India's legal-aid cases at ₹500-3,000 per matter. They can't afford tools. We're free for them because (a) it's the right thing to do, and (b) you're our distribution channel into India's mid-tier criminal bar.
Can I use my own LLM (Ollama, Llama, etc)?
Enterprise only. Set LLM_PROVIDER=ollama and we route your queries to your local model instead of Groq/Gemini.
What about data residency?
Default = AWS Mumbai (ap-south-1). Enterprise customers can choose on-prem, AWS Hyderabad, or your own VPC.
