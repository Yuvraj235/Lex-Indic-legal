The Transition

163 years of IPC, replaced overnight.

On 1 July 2024, the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita replaced the Indian Penal Code. Every advocate in India is now working off two parallel codes — the IPC they trained on, and the BNS they're expected to file under.

358
BNS sections
A new code, with renumbered sections, new offences (snatching, organised crime, mob lynching), and updated punishments. Every IPC reference in your old drafts is now stale.
~30 min
Per-case manual lookup
Cross-referencing a fact pattern against the bare act, picking the right charging sections, and drafting the FIR by hand. Multiplied by every case in your matter book.
0%
Trust in raw LLMs
Generic chatbots hallucinate section numbers and punishments. No advocate can sign their bar number on output they cannot trace back to the authoritative bare act.
How it works

Three steps. One brief. Every citation verified.

Lex-Indic is a retrieval-augmented engine: we never let the model invent law. The relevant BNS sections are pulled from a curated knowledge base first, then handed to the model with strict instructions to cite only what it was given.

01
📝

Capture the story

Type, paste, or dictate the client's account. Attach evidence (PDFs, screenshots, CCTV stills) — Gemini Vision extracts text and notes the visual context.

02
🔎

Retrieve the law

ChromaDB vector search finds the most relevant BNS sections, IPC→BNS mappings, and Supreme Court precedents from a curated KB of 38 sections + 61 corpus documents.

03
⚖️

Generate the brief

Six sections, court-ready: Immediate Advisory · Legal Analysis · Strategy · Draft FIR · Legal Notice · Police Help Report. Plus a 9-page branded PDF case brief.

The moat

Every claim. Every citation. Traceable.

Click any "BNS Section 304" mention in the analysis to see the verified bare-act-style entry that produced it — including IPC replaced, punishment, bailable status, and a deep-link to indiacode.nic.in.

✓

✓
Refusal on out-of-KB queries. Ask LEXI about a section we don't have? It refuses and points you to the bare act instead of inventing.
✓

Compliance

Built for the firm GC's first question.

"Where does the data live, and can we audit who saw what?" Three foundations that turn this from a tool into something procurement will sign.

🔐

Hashed PII in audit

Every request is logged with a SHA-256 hash of the client story (deployment-salted), never the raw text. DPDP Act minimisation principle, satisfied.

📜

Append-only audit trail

JSONL daily-rotated logs with request_id, retrieved sections, latency, status. One cron job ships them to your SIEM. The CLI report comes built-in.

🇮🇳

India-region ready

Single Flask process. Run on your AWS Mumbai box, or self-host Llama-3.3-70B via Ollama for client-confidential matters. No data leaves your VPC.
