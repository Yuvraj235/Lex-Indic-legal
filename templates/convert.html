Why this matters Indian advocates have a stack of IPC-era drafts that need rewriting under the BNS, which replaced the 163-year-old Indian Penal Code on 1 July 2024. Manual cross-reference takes ~5 minutes per document. This tool does the safe parts (51 high-confidence IPC→BNS mappings) automatically, and flags everything else for your review. Sections we don't have a verified mapping for stay plain text — your visual signal that the converter has not silently invented a section number.
