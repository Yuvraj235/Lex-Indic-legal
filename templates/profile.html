⚠ Danger zone — Right to be forgotten (DPDP §13)

You can request permanent deletion of your account and all associated data. A 7-day grace period applies so you can change your mind. After 7 days the data is irreversibly removed and the audit-log salt is rotated so historical entries can no longer be linked back to you.

What gets deleted: account, API keys, NALSA registration, lead capture entry.
What stays (legal records): Matters created by you are soft-preserved per Bar Council rules but become unlinked from your identity.
